The problem

Nobody touched your app. It broke anyway.

Every September

A new version of iOS ships, and every phone updates itself. An app built for last year's version starts behaving badly, or stops opening at all.

Without warning

Apple and Google set compliance deadlines. The notice goes to a developer account nobody checks any more. Miss it and the app comes down.

Quietly

Certificates expire. The payment, booking or map service inside your app drops support for old versions. One morning the button just doesn't work.

And then

Your app slides down the search results, your reviews turn, and the people who would have booked with you book with someone else.

None of this starts with something you did. All of it comes with a deadline.
